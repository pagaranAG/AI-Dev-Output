About Sisig House

Celebrating Filipino culinary heritage through every sizzling plate

🇵🇭 Our Story

Sisig House was founded in 2015 by a family passionate about sharing authentic Filipino cuisine with the world. What started as a small food cart in Calamba has grown into a beloved restaurant chain with 6 thriving locations across Laguna Province.

Our founder, Maria Santos, grew up watching her grandmother prepare traditional Filipino dishes. Inspired by these memories, Maria decided to bring the sizzling, aromatic, and mouthwatering experience of Filipino home cooking to restaurants.

Today, Sisig House remains committed to using the freshest ingredients, honoring traditional cooking methods, and creating an atmosphere where families and friends can gather to celebrate good food and Filipino culture.

🎯 Our Mission

Quality: We believe in using only the finest ingredients. Our pork is sourced from trusted local farms, our seafood is fresh daily, and our spices are carefully selected.

Authenticity: Every dish on our menu honors traditional Filipino recipes while adding our unique house twist to create unforgettable flavors.

Community: We're more than a restaurant - we're a gathering place where memories are made, families bond, and the Filipino spirit shines through shared meals.

Excellence: Our chefs continuously train and innovate to bring you the best dining experience. We treat every customer like family.
